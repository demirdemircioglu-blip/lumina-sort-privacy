Privacy Policy (English)

Thank you for playing Lumina Sort.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected and used when you use the Lumina Sort mobile application.

Information We Collect

Lumina Sort does not require account registration and does not directly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, address, or phone number.

The game may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Current level progress
  • Coin balance
  • Game settings
  • Audio preferences
  • Theme and customization selections

This information is stored locally on your device using local storage and is used only to provide game functionality.

Advertising

Lumina Sort may display advertisements through Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may collect and process certain information in accordance with its own privacy policies, including:

  • Device information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Diagnostic information
  • Usage information related to advertisements

For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Analytics

Lumina Sort may use analytics services to understand how players use the game and to improve gameplay experience.

Analytics data may include:

  • Level completion statistics
  • Game performance information
  • Device type and operating system
  • Crash reports

This information is used only to improve the game.

Data Storage

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

If you uninstall the application, locally stored data may be removed.

Children's Privacy

Lumina Sort is intended for a general audience.

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe personal information has been collected unintentionally, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services including:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Services

These services operate under their own privacy policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

Any updates will be posted within the application or on the official privacy policy page.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact: demwares@gmail.com
